When Odoo receives a mail on a record, it forwards a copy to every
follower of the record, sent as ``<sender name> <catchall address>``.

This copy:

- leaks replies meant for the company only to the other external
  followers;
- is flagged as impersonation by some mail servers (e.g. Microsoft
  Defender).

This module stops forwarding incoming mails to the followers by email.
The mail is still logged in the chatter, and followers notified in their
Odoo inbox are still notified.

Configuration
=============

Go to *Settings > General Settings > Discuss*:

- *Don't forward incoming mails to followers*: enabled by default.
  Disable it to get the standard Odoo behaviour back.
- *Email internal users about incoming mails*: only visible when the
  forward is disabled. Enabled by default: internal followers notified
  by email keep receiving the incoming mails.
